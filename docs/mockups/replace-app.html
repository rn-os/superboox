Replace an app at the bottom

  • Settings › Apps at the bottom › ✎ on an app opens Edit app (today's Rename dialog).
  • New Replace app… button in that dialog opens the usual app list.
  • The app you pick takes the old one's place: same spot in the row, nothing to move with ↑ ↓.
  • It shows the new app's own name. A custom name on the old app isn't carried over.
  • Name, Reset, Cancel and Save work as before. "Previous app" has no ✎, so it can't be replaced.
  • Black and white only; nothing changes on monochrome screens.
