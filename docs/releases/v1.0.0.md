**SuperBoox 1.0.0**: the first stable release. A Supernote-style Quick Access panel for BOOX: swipe the screen edge on any page.

**What's in it**
- **Quick Access:** pinned notes, books, folders and apps. Tap a pinned note and it opens straight in BOOX Notes, even from inside another note.
- **Last opened** note and document. **Recent** files.
- **Clipboard history:** text, links and images, plus handwriting you lasso-copy in BOOX Notes, shown as a picture. Search, filter, pin, copy again, paste in app, share.
- **Quick Controls:** Wi‑Fi, Bluetooth, Do Not Disturb, Auto-rotate, Full Refresh and more.
- **Front Light presets:** BOOX's own presets, plus your own named presets (with Advanced access). The panel stays in sync with BOOX's Control Center.
- **New Note:** tap for a new note. Hold to open BOOX's create-note screen on your recent templates. Backing out returns you where you were.
- **First-run tour, live previews in Settings, Backup & restore.**
- **Reliability:** errors in Settings or the panel are recovered from without stopping the service. Settings shows if the service has stopped and can restart it.

**Install:** download the `.apk` below on your BOOX and open it, or add `https://github.com/rn-os/superboox` in Obtainium. Then follow the tour.
No adb or computer needed. No internet permission.

Tested on a Note Air 6C (Android 16, firmware 4.3). **Found a bug?** SuperBoox → Troubleshooting → Copy debug report.
