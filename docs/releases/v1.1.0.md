**SuperBoox 1.1.0**: better support for older BOOX models and firmware, plus fixes from your reports.

**Older BOOX firmware (Note Air 2 and similar)**
- Pinned notes open from anywhere, not only while the Notes library is showing. SuperBoox finds the launcher's Notes tab even when it has only icons, learns which one it is, and goes into the folders a note is in.
- **Add** works in the Notes library (opens the picker) and inside an open note.
- New notes show up under **Last opened** note.

**Everywhere**
- **Add on BOOX's Library tab** lets you pick books to pin. They open straight in NeoReader.
- **Clipboard:** with All files access, every lasso copy from BOOX Notes is kept as you make it, not only the last one before the panel opens. Handwriting previews show the writing again.
- **Older handwriting:** choose any earlier handwriting clip and BOOX Notes pastes it as handwriting, not as a text link, even after BOOX has removed its own copy.
- **Clear the clipboard from the panel:** a **Clear** button beside the clipboard's title empties the history after a confirm. Pinned clips stay.
- **Recent Files:** an × on each row takes just that one off the list, and **Clear all** at the bottom empties it after a confirm. Only the list changes; no note or file is deleted.
- **Clipboard settings, tidier:** one **File access** card (handwriting previews and screenshots) instead of two places to allow All files access, and **Every copy, as it happens** only shows when Advanced access is connected.
- **Troubleshooting, all in one place:** Settings → Help → Troubleshooting has the debug report, your BOOX model and firmware, and which features have been confirmed on it. Not confirmed yet? A debug report is what gets it checked.
- **Settings sized for your screen:** Settings scales itself for 7", 10.3" and 13.3" tablets (a little more on color screens) and no longer shrinks with a system font below 100%. Troubleshooting → Screen has a manual Settings size.
- **Writing mode:** a new Quick Control (add it in Settings → Quick Controls). One tap turns off Wi‑Fi, Bluetooth and the Front Light; tap again and only what it switched comes back, with the light on the preset it was on. No extra permission.
- **What's new:** updating from 1.0.0 shows three short pages once (Settings → Help → What's new in 1.1.0 shows them again).
- **Swipes in the panel:** swipe right on Quick Access for Recent Files; in the Clipboard swipe left for older clips, right for newer.
- **Front Light tile** cycles Off, Soft, Bright and Custom in one tap.
- **Apps at the bottom** fit your pinned apps automatically; **Previous app** always sits bottom-right.
- **Notes folders:** pin a folder and expand it in the panel; Last Opened Note shows its folder path.
- **Clipboard names and Mark up:** name and pin handwriting clips, search by name, mark up a picture in BOOX's editor, and share an image from any app to **Add to Clipboard**. Tap a clip to paste it; hold for its actions.
- **Writing card:** a "Writing in Notes?" card in BOOX Notes offers the Writing button.
- **Settings cleanup:** fewer pages and switches, nothing greyed out; **Show tips again** is under Help.
- **This app:** a strip above the apps names the app that's open. **Optimize** opens BOOX's Customize panel (refresh, display and, on color screens, color) for that app, and **EinkWise** opens BOOX's E-Ink Center, both right over the app. Changes apply while it runs. Turn it off in Settings → Apps at the bottom.
- **Hold Last Opened:** hold Last Opened Note to open BOOX Notes' library in that note's folder; hold Last Opened Document to open its app (for NeoReader, BOOX's Library).
- **Replace an app at the bottom:** Settings → Apps at the bottom → ✎ → **Replace app…** swaps in another app in the same spot.
- **Gesture settings** explain that the edge marker stays thin while the whole strip responds.

**Install:** download the `.apk` below on your BOOX and open it, or add `https://github.com/rn-os/superboox` in Obtainium.
No adb or computer needed. No internet permission.

**Found a bug?** SuperBoox → Troubleshooting → Copy debug report.
