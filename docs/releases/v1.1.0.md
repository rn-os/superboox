**SuperBoox 1.1.0**: better support for older BOOX models and firmware, plus fixes from your reports.

**Older BOOX firmware (Note Air 2 and similar)**
- Pinned notes open from anywhere, not only while the Notes library is showing. SuperBoox finds the launcher's Notes tab even when it has only icons, learns which one it is, and goes into the folders a note is in.
- **Add** works in the Notes library (opens the picker) and inside an open note.
- New notes show up under **Last opened** note.

**Everywhere**
- **Add on BOOX's Library tab** lets you pick books to pin. They open straight in NeoReader.
- **Clipboard:** with All files access, every lasso copy from BOOX Notes is kept as you make it, not only the last one before the panel opens. Handwriting previews show the writing again.
- **Older handwriting:** choose any earlier handwriting clip and BOOX Notes pastes it as handwriting, not as a text link, even after BOOX has removed its own copy.
- **Clear the clipboard from the panel:** a **Clear** button beside the clipboard's title empties the history after a confirm. Pinned clips stay.
- **Recent Files:** an × on each row takes just that one off the list, and **Clear all** at the bottom empties it after a confirm. Only the list changes; no note or file is deleted.
- **Your device:** the tour and Settings → Help → Your device show your BOOX model and firmware and which features have been confirmed on it. Not confirmed yet? A debug report is what gets it checked.
- **Writing mode:** a new Quick Control (add it in Settings → Quick Controls). One tap turns off Wi‑Fi, Bluetooth and the Front Light; tap again and only what it switched comes back, with the light on the preset it was on. No extra permission.
- **Gesture settings** explain that the edge marker stays thin while the whole strip responds.

**Install:** download the `.apk` below on your BOOX and open it, or add `https://github.com/rn-os/superboox` in Obtainium.
No adb or computer needed. No internet permission.

**Found a bug?** SuperBoox → Troubleshooting → Copy debug report.
