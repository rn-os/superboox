**SuperBoox 1.1.2**: the Split Screen Quick Control works again.

- **Split Screen** in Quick Controls did nothing on Android 12 and later: Android ignores the split-screen action it used. It now presses BOOX's own Split Screen tile in the Control Center, like the other BOOX tiles, and falls back to Android's action on firmware without that tile. Hold it to open the tile's own options.

Checked on a Note Air 6C (Android 16, firmware 4.3).

**Install:** download the `.apk` below on your BOOX and open it, or add `https://github.com/rn-os/superboox` in Obtainium.
No adb or computer needed. No internet permission.

**Found a bug?** SuperBoox → Troubleshooting → Copy debug report.
